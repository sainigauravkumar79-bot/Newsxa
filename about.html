Our story

About Newsxa

Newsxa was built on a simple idea: the news cycle moves faster than any single newsroom, so the fastest way to stay informed is to bring the best of many newsrooms into one clean, fast place.

What we do

Newsxa is an aggregator, not a publisher. Every headline, image, and excerpt on this site is pulled live from the RSS and API feeds of established news organizations and links straight back to the original article on the publisher's own site. We don't write news, and we never fabricate it.

How it stays current

Our sections refresh automatically every few minutes, pulling directly from source feeds so what you see reflects what's actually being published right now — not a cached snapshot from hours ago.

Our sources

We currently connect to BBC News, The New York Times, and Google News' regional editions, with room to add more providers as our source list grows. See the full list on our RSS Sources page.
