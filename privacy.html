Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Newsxa. We've kept the site's data footprint deliberately small.

What we store locally

Your theme choice (light/dark), selected country edition, and any bookmarked articles are saved only in your browser's local storage. This information never leaves your device and isn't transmitted to Newsxa's servers.

Third-party content requests

To display live headlines, your browser requests data from RSS-to-JSON conversion services and the original publishers' feeds (BBC, The New York Times, Google News, and similar). These requests are subject to those providers' own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

Newsxa does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to disclose exactly what's measured.

Children's privacy

Newsxa is a general-audience news product and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date below whenever this policy changes materially.
